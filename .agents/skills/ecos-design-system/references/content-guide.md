# Content and voice

Write in Brazilian Portuguese unless the user explicitly requests another language.

## Voice

Ecos sounds clear, confident, collaborative, and concise. It speaks as an experienced partner, not as an impersonal software factory. Prefer concrete verbs such as **entender**, **desenhar**, **construir**, **evoluir** and **transformar**.

## Message structure

- Lead with the visitor's outcome.
- Explain the approach without inflated technical language.
- Keep headings short and memorable.
- Use the echo metaphor selectively. Repeating it in every section weakens the concept.
- Calls to action should feel direct and conversational: “Vamos conversar” or “Comece um projeto”.

## Claims

Do not invent clients, case studies, team size, years of experience, awards, delivery statistics, testimonials, addresses, social profiles, or certifications. Mark business details as pending when they were not supplied.

## Terminology

Preferred terms include “produto digital”, “experiência”, “estratégia”, “design”, “desenvolvimento”, “evolução” and “parceria”. Avoid buzzword chains, superlatives without evidence, and English words when a natural Portuguese equivalent exists.
